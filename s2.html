Course ID: 09ENG • Language & Literature (MYP Year 4)

Unit 2: Songs & Poetry of Conflict — Critical Aesthetics

Statement of Inquiry: "A critical understanding of aesthetics (structure, style, and craft) enhances our appreciation of artistry and creation." We examine how poetic perspective communicates the stark realities of war through Wilfred Owen and Rupert Brooke.

Idealism & Form

Rupert Brooke — The Soldier

Petrarchan sonnet structure framing pastoral English identity, sacrifice, and honor amidst foreign soil.

Visceral Realism

Wilfred Owen — Dulce et Decorum Est

Broken iambic metre, sensory imagery, and bitter rejection of the ancient lie regarding martial glory.

Syllabus & Core Texts: Perspectives, Aesthetics & Culture

Exploring how cultural and historical contexts shape personal ambition, poetic craft, and communal identities—from Lorraine Hansberry's Chicago to Wilfred Owen's trenches and Yukio Mishima's coastal tides.

Unit 1: Drama & Context

A Raisin in the Sun & Of Mice and Men

Statement of Inquiry: The value and purpose of dreams and ambition is dependent on historical, social and cultural contexts.

Unit 2: Aesthetics & Craft

Songs & Poetry of Conflict

Statement of Inquiry: A critical understanding of aesthetics (structure, style, and craft) enhances our appreciation of artistry and creation.

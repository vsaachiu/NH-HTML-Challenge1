Course ID: 09ENG • Aberdeen Maritime Lore & Coastal Ballads

The Sea Sanctuaries: Corsair Verse & Coastal Ballads

From the mist-shrouded granite slopes of Nam Long Shan (Brick Hill) to the moorings of the Aberdeen South Channel, this course investigates the intersection between 19th-century romantic poetry and the oral traditions of the South China coastal fleets.

We shall explore how Byron, Shelley, and ancient Tanka fishing ballads documented the sanctuary accords of 1809, preserved beneath the ancient Tin Hau shrine before our permanent Shum Wan campus was even conceived.
